# Send & track: how to send parcels

This is the one page you need for orders and deliveries. Open **Admin › Sales › Send & track**, or **Quick actions › Send a parcel**.

## Send a WhatsApp order

1. Press **New order**.
2. Fill in the customer, the items, the address and the delivery option, the same way as before.
3. Press **Save and send to …**. The button names the courier.

Goreto then accepts the order and sends it to the courier of the delivery option you picked.

- **Daraz Express:** the parcel is booked straight away and you get a tracking number. Press **Print label & call pickup**, stick the label on the parcel, and Daraz comes to collect it.
- **Any other courier:** press **Send on WhatsApp**. WhatsApp opens with the order details already written, and you tap Send.

You can leave the **Parcel weight** box empty. Goreto then uses the product weights, or the usual parcel weight if the products have none. Daraz weighs every parcel again at pickup.

## Track parcels

Every order is listed under **Parcels**, newest first. Each row shows:

- how far the parcel has got (Accepted → Sent to courier → On the way → Delivered);
- the latest update from the courier;
- **one button** for the next thing to do, when something needs doing.

To find one parcel, type its order number, the customer's phone or name, or the tracking number in **Find a parcel**.

Daraz updates arrive by themselves. When you open the page, Goreto also checks Daraz for any parcel that hasn't been updated in the last 15 minutes. **Refresh tracking** checks them all again.

## When a row asks for something

| The row says | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| New order, waiting for you | Press **Accept & send to …** |
| Accepted, not booked with Daraz yet | Press **Book with Daraz** |
| Booked with Daraz | Press **Print label & call pickup** once the parcel is packed |
| Daraz couldn't deliver it | Call the customer, then press **Try again or return** |
| Daraz needs the parcel weight | Press **Add the weight**, or set a usual parcel weight under Autopilot |
| … has no WhatsApp number | Press **Add the number** and save the courier's WhatsApp number |
| Returned to the store | Open the order, then cancel it so the stock comes back |
| Waiting for the Daraz connection | Nothing to do. Daraz parcels are booked once the Daraz keys are added. |

## Autopilot

Autopilot is at the bottom of the page, and only the owner can change it. When it's **on**:

- every new order, from the website or typed in, is accepted straight away;
- it goes to the courier of the delivery option the customer chose;
- Daraz parcels are booked automatically.

You only print labels and tap **Send on WhatsApp** for the other couriers.

The **Ready?** list shows anything that would stop Autopilot from working. Set **Usual parcel weight** (for example 500 g) so products without a weight can still be booked.

Turn Autopilot **off** if you want to check every order before it goes out. New orders then wait in the list with an **Accept & send** button.
